Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our Makkah food delivery service.

Do you deliver food to hotels in Makkah?

Yes. We provide food delivery to hotels and customer locations in central Makkah. Send us your hotel name, room number and Google Maps location through WhatsApp.

Can I order from any restaurant in Makkah?

Yes. You can request food from your favorite restaurant in Makkah. If the restaurant is not listed on our website, simply tell us the restaurant name and your food order.

Do you deliver food near Makkah Clock Tower?

Yes. We focus on central Makkah delivery areas including Makkah Clock Tower, Ajyad, Jabal Omar, Ibrahim Al Khalil and nearby hotels.

How much does food delivery cost in Makkah?

Our service and delivery fee starts from 30 SAR. The restaurant food price is separate and will be confirmed before we purchase the order.

How can I order food through WhatsApp?

Fill in the order form on our website with your name, WhatsApp number, restaurant, food order, hotel and location. Then review your order and send it directly to our WhatsApp.

Can I send my Google Maps location?

Yes. You can paste your Google Maps link in the order form. This helps us find your hotel or delivery location in Makkah.

Do you have your own restaurant?

No. Makkah Delivery Hub is a food purchasing and delivery service. We buy food from the restaurant you choose and deliver it to your hotel or location.

How long does food delivery take?

Delivery time depends on the restaurant, food preparation time, traffic and your location. You can tell us your preferred delivery time when placing your order.
